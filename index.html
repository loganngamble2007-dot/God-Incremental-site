Game Guide

New to God Incremental? Open the guide for a simple overview of progression and the major systems.

Open the beginner's guide
Tiers & progression

Build your stats with training and upgrades, then meet the requirements shown in-game to advance through tiers. The current update history lists 100 max tiers, with tiers 51–100 added in Update 6.

When progress slows, check your available upgrades, achievements, skill tree, and newly unlocked maps before moving on. Exact tier requirements can change between updates, so use the in-game tier screen for current values.

God Power, Rebirths & other resets

Use each reset system when its requirements are met. Rebirths and Sacrifices are part of the progression, while Super Prestige has three progressive tiers and Ascension resets cash for global stat multipliers.

God Power and the exact reset costs, rewards, and what each reset removes should be checked in their in-game menus. Read the confirmation screen before resetting so you know what progress will be lost and what permanent bonus you will gain.

Stats, currencies & upgrades

Train and upgrade your stats to earn more cash and meet progression requirements. Other systems may add their own resources, including Fantasies, leaves, mining ores, Sugar, and Candy Canes.

Check the sidebar, Shop, skill tree, and the menu for each unlocked map to see your current totals, multipliers, and available upgrades. Resource uses and unlock requirements are shown in-game and can change with updates.

Runes & their boosts

Runes provide bonuses and are connected to different progression systems. The update history mentions Fantasy Runes, Leaf Runes, six Sugar Runes, and the Pumpkin Rune from the seasonal event.

Maps, mining, events & codes

Unlock maps as you progress. The Cave map includes mining ores and pickaxe upgrades; the Candy map features Sugar Dice, Sugar, Candy Canes, and Sugar Runes. Seasonal maps and events can add limited-time resources and rewards.

Redeem active codes at the bottom of the in-game Shop. Check achievements and the skill tree for additional goals and upgrades.

Game systems and unlock requirements may change with updates. Follow the in-game instructions for exact requirements.

How to Play: Maps 1–5
Map 1 — Main (Hub World)
  • Walk around to auto-collect God Power (GP) orbs as they spawn.
  • Spend GP on the upgrade boards: GP Per Orb, Pickup Range, Walk Speed, Max Orbs, Spawn Speed, and GP Multiplier.
  • Climb the 100-tier rank ladder by spending GP. Tier 1 starts at 8,000 GP.
  • At Tier 3+, use the Rebirth Station to reset GP and upgrades for rebirth points; spend those on Rebirth Upgrades and the Rebirth Tree.
  • Stand on the Rune Station pad to auto-roll Bulk, Luck, and Speed runes for permanent boosts. Use the Rarity Roller for extra multipliers.
  • Later unlocks include the Crown Collector (passive crowns), Sacrifice Station (at 1 trillion GP), Diamond Shop, and God Power Arena.
Map 2 — Forest (World 2)
  • Unlock this world through the World 2 Portal once your tier is high enough.
  • Buy Dropper 1 (10 billion crowns) to start earning Fantasies, then buy Dropper 2 and the Upgrader to multiply earnings.
  • Buy Fantasy Upgrades: Fantasy Gain, Drop Speed, Upgrader Power, Crown Boost, and GP Boost.
  • Use the Fantasy Rune Station for map-specific runes.
  • At high Fantasy counts, use Super Prestige to reset stats for permanent multipliers: 1.5x at 1B, 3x at 100B, and 5x at 1T Fantasies. Tiers, runes, diamonds, and rarity are preserved.
Map 3 — Cave (World 3)
  • Enter through the World 3 Portal; you automatically receive a Pickaxe tool.
  • Click ores to mine them: Coal (most common, lowest value), Iron, Gold, and Diamond (rarest, most valuable).
  • Sell ores at the Sell Station for Mining Cash.
  • Buy Mining Upgrades: Ore Luck, Backpack Capacity, Ore Yield, Sell Bonus, and Auto Mine.
  • Spend cash and ores in the Cave Perk Tree and on the Pickaxe Evolution Board to upgrade pickaxe power and appearance.
  • Buy Ascension tiers (I through V) for permanent earnings multipliers (up to 15x) with no reset.
Map 4 — Fall Event
  • This is the Halloween event map. Collect leaves and progress through Leaf Ranks; the Fall Rune Station requires Leaf Rank 10.
  • Stand on rune station pads to roll event runes. A separate Pumpkin Rune Station uses Pumpkins, with one Pumpkin per roll.
  • Event availability and unlock details may change; check the in-game map and station requirements.
Map 5 — Candy (World 4)
  • Unlock at Tier 70 (“Rank LXX: The Everything”).
  • Click the Sugar Dice Board to roll dice. Payouts scale with the pip total, with bonuses for doubles and a jackpot for double sixes.
  • Buy Sugar Upgrades to improve rolls: sugar per pip, six chance, doubles multiplier, jackpot multiplier, surge, and tier bonus.
  • Upgrade to up to 6 dice for bigger rolls.
  • Collect Candy Canes for sugar multipliers, use the Candy Rune Station for runes, and convert between currencies with the Sugar Conversion system.

General tips for every map

  • Walk near orbs and collectibles to auto-pick them up; pickup-range upgrades make this much easier.
  • Stand on rune station pads in each map to passively roll that map’s runes.
  • Use portals to travel between worlds; they have a short cooldown.
  • The StatsHUD tracks your currencies, and the Tier ProgressBar shows rank progress.
How to Complete the Halloween Map

Map 4 — Fall Event. This was the 2026 Halloween event, which ended on November 20, 2026. During the event, players could enter through the Fall Event Portal with no tier or level requirement. The map may no longer be available.

Step 1 — Collect leaves

Leaves fall around the map and are collected automatically when you walk nearby. Start with Maple Leaves (×1 value), then unlock rarer leaf types. The map has 40 leaves at a time, increased to 100 with Gale upgrades, and leaves respawn every 3 seconds.

Step 5 — Evil Pumpkin boss (optional)

Stand within 18 studs to automatically attack the Evil Pumpkin. It has 500 HP and respawns in 4 seconds. Deal at least 50 damage (10% of its HP) before it dies to earn Pumpkins; the base reward is 10 per kill.

Spend Pumpkins on boss upgrades:

  • Pumpkin Damage (max 50): +25% damage per level
  • Attack Speed (max 35): +15% attack speed per level
  • Respawn Speed (max 20): −10% respawn time per level
  • More Pumpkins (max 350): +10% Pumpkins per kill

Use 1 Pumpkin per roll at the Pumpkin Rune Station for Gourd, Jack, Hollow, and Reaper runes.

Step 6 — Fall Rune Station

Unlocks at Leaf Rank 10. Stand on the rune pad to auto-roll for 1,000 Leaves per roll. There are 13 event runes, from Fall Rune to Omega Fall Rune; maxing Omega Fall Rune grants 10× God Power. Tempest and Sovereign Fall Runes boost non-event rune stations by up to ×2.5 and ×3, respectively.

Completion checklist & tips

  • Reach Rank 15, “The Last Leaf” (1.5T lifetime leaves).
  • Unlock all 10 leaf mods, through Leaf of Infinity.
  • Max key upgrades: Leaf Value, Leaf Surge, Leaf Storm, and Divine Luck.
  • Farm the Evil Pumpkin for Pumpkins and Pumpkin Runes.
  • Roll event runes after reaching Rank 10.
  • Rank up, then quickly rebuy mods and upgrades; this is the core progression loop.
  • Prioritize Leaf Value, Leaf Surge, and Leaf Storm to grow your income.
  • Leaf Magnet and Mega Magnet speed up collection; higher ranks improve rare leaf chance.
  • The 2026 Halloween event ended on November 20, 2026; event access may depend on whether it returns in a future update.

Estimated Time to Complete Each System

Note: These are estimates for an active player who buys upgrades as they go. Actual times vary considerably depending on multipliers, rebirths, and runes.

Map 1 — Main
  • Tier 1 (8K GP): ~10–20 minutes. Base income is ~2 GP/sec; GP Per Orb and GP Multiplier upgrades speed this up quickly.
  • Tier 3 (Rebirth unlock): ~30–60 minutes; Tier 5: ~1–2 hours.
  • Tier 10 (15Qa GP): a few hours with rebirths boosting you.
  • Tier 22 (Poseidon rune unlock): several hours of rebirth cycles.
  • Tier 70 (Candy map unlock): days of play, with rebirths, sacrifices, and runes stacking.
  • Tier 100 (3.3e133 GP): weeks of stacked multipliers. Tier bonuses multiply orb value up to 4.5e49, so income grows with you.
  • Max all 6 upgrade boards: a few hours early on. Costs are linear, but Spawn Speed and Max Orbs unlock later.
  • Max Rebirth Upgrades (all 10 levels): depends on rebirth income; final levels cost 2,000+ rebirth points each.
  • Max Rebirth Tree pads: mixed. Early pads cost 250K–25M GP (hours); rune pads cost 1e18–1e20 GP and 1B rebirths (very late game).
  • Max Mystery Upgrades (5 levels each, 50–800 points): several rebirth cycles.
  • Sacrifice Station: unlocks at 1 trillion rebirths; deep endgame content.
Map 2 — Forest
  • Dropper 1 (10B Crowns): depends on Crown Collector level. Base income is ~2 crowns/sec (10 per 5 sec); expect hours of crown farming after Crown Ranks (2x per rank) and Crown upgrades.
  • Dropper 2 + Upgrader (500 Fantasies each): ~30–60 minutes after Dropper 1.
  • Max Fantasy Upgrades: a few hours of dropper income.
  • Super Prestige I (1B Fantasies): days of dropper income with upgrades; II (100B): significantly longer; III (1T): weeks and the map’s endgame.
Map 3 — Cave
  • First ores to first pickaxe evolution (Iron Pickaxe, 2.5K cash): ~15–30 minutes. Coal takes 2 clicks at base damage and gives 8 cash.
  • All 5 Pickaxe Evolutions (up to Mythic, 20M cash + 30 Diamonds): several hours.
  • Max Mining Upgrades: hours of mining; Auto Mine at 25K cash is the big unlock.
  • Cave Perk Tree maxed: several hours; later perks need Gold and Diamonds.
  • Ascension I (25M cash): ~2–4 hours with upgrades; II (200M): several more hours; III (2B): about a day; IV (20B): days; V (200B): a week or more.
  • Cave Runes maxed: Quartz fills fast. The tuning estimates Amethyst every 2–3 opens, Onyx ~7, Emerald ~20, Sapphire ~60, and Heartstone ~150. Maxing all six takes a few hours on the pad (3-second roll cooldown, down to 0.25 seconds).
Map 4 — Fall Event (Halloween)
  • Rank 1 (750 lifetime leaves): a few minutes; Rank 5 (375K): ~10–15 minutes with leaf upgrades.
  • Rank 10 (750M, Fall Rune Station unlock): ~30–40 minutes.
  • Rank 15, “The Last Leaf” (1.5T lifetime leaves): ~1 hour, based on the tuning goal for the final rank.
  • Max the 6 main event runes (Fall, Harvest, Equinox, Eternal Fall, Aurora, Primordial Fall): ~45 minutes on the pad, or about an hour including leaf income.
  • Unlock all 10 Leaf Mods through Leaf of Infinity (100B leaves): happens naturally on the way to Rank 15, about an hour total.
  • Evil Pumpkin first kill: seconds (500 HP, 25 damage per 0.8 seconds). Max Pumpkin upgrades depends on kills; 350 levels of More Pumpkins makes it a long passive grind.
Map 5 — Candy
  • Map unlock: Tier 70; see Map 1 for the days-long progression estimate.
  • First Candy Canes (25K sugar each): ~10–15 minutes of dice rolling (about 70 sugar per roll at base, 1 roll per second).
  • Max Sugar Upgrades: several hours of rolling. Extra Dice starts at 150K sugar and costs 8x more per level.
  • Max Candy Canes / full conversion bonuses: days of rolling and converting.

Overall game completion

  • Fall Event map: ~1–1.5 hours (designed to be the fastest map to complete).
  • Cave map (Ascension V + runes): ~1–2 weeks of casual play.
  • Forest map (Super Prestige III): weeks.
  • Main map Tier 100: weeks to months; the true endgame.
  • Candy map: gated behind Tier 70, so it is late-game content by default.
